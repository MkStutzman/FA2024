dark mode is more than just an aesthetic choice its also a choice for users who may otherwise have visual impedaments. Dark mode works by blackening the background of a website/app while lightening or whitening its elements. For example instagram when switched to dark mode its background becomes black and it elements like the like, share, coment/comments are all changed to white. making the more important elements stand out.

Nonstandard Scrolling Reading Response

I strongly dislike websites like this. There are in some cases I believe where it can be really cool and add a lot of character and emotion to the website and user experience itself but, when you are using these scolded animated effects that move as you move, it really distracts my eye and makes the website feel “loose”. Meaning i feel like if i scroll too hard i'm going to scroll off the page. I guess another word to describe what I mean is “bouncy”. I don't know, if I'm scrolling through a website and trying to read and gather information I'd rather just scroll up and down rather than scroll and wait for it to appear through a slide in animation. Another thing that just gets me about this is that if you scroll the slightest bit too far the information will go away or animate back out. It's just touchy. I don't like it. But I like the scrolling functions for images or animated elements. I like the way Apple's website uses it to showcase the airpods and animates moving images/ functions rather than written information. The lirix website threw me off. I feel like I'm physically getting information thrown at me.
